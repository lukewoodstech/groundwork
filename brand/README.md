# Groundwork brand

- `mark.svg`: the mark. A block of ground with one path cut through it. White on near black, 32 unit grid, no gradients.
- `lockup.svg`: mark plus wordmark as drawn in the app header (`components/brand/Logo.tsx`).
- `mark-a-grid.svg`, `mark-b-strata.svg`, `mark-c-ground.svg`: the three options considered. `index.html` compares them at 16 to 512px. C was chosen for reading best at 16px.
- Tagline: "See where you've been. Know where to go."
- Colour lives only on the map: amber `rgb(226 152 62)` for Us, steel blue `rgb(102 136 176)` for Previous elders.

Icons and the social image are generated from `mark.svg` into `app/` and `public/`.
